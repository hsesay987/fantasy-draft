# Say Less Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship Say Less, a word-budget describing party game, in party rooms (phones, screen host, teams, tournaments, optional buzz mode).

**Architecture:** A pure engine `app/topicDraft/formats/sayless.ts` owns turns, clues, guesses, budget, scoring and timing; the server runs it like Vowel Play and sends the secret word only to the describer's seat. Word decks are data behind a registry (`sayless/source.ts`, like `party/vowelSource.ts`) so room bundles never carry words. No solo mode and no daily (out of scope in the spec).

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 + React 19 web, Express 5 + Socket.IO backend with in-memory rooms, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-new-games-design.md`, section 2 and "Shared rules".

## Global Constraints

- Format id `"sayless"`, title "Say Less". Joins `PARTY_FORMATS` and the server's `FORMAT_IDS`. 2+ players.
- Turn: the describer sees the secret word and its par; nobody else sees the word, and neither does the screen or a seat with no token, until the word ends.
- Clues go one word at a time. A clue is one word with no spaces (use `normalizeClue` from `app/topicDraft/party/clueText.ts`: collapse spaces, max 40 chars). It is refused, and the describer must send a different clue, when:
  - it contains the secret word, or any part of a multi-word answer that has 3 or more letters;
  - the secret word contains the clue and the clue has 4 or more letters;
  - the clue is a plural of the secret word (word + "s"/"es", y→"ies") or the word is a plural of the clue.
  Comparison is on normalised text (`normName`-style: case, accents, punctuation, spaces, leading "the" removed).
- Guessers type guesses at any time. A guess matches on normalised text plus the word's stored aliases. Guess text max 60 chars, 3 guesses per second per seat.
- Word ends on the first right guess, when the describer passes, or after `WORD_MS = 60_000`.
- Scoring: the first right guesser scores `max(1, par + 2 − clues)`; the describer scores the same amount. Example: par 3, guess after 2 clues → 3 each; after 5 clues → 1 each. Nobody scores when nobody gets the word.
- Rounds: each player describes once per round; host sets 1 to 3 (default 2). Words per turn: host sets 3 to 5 (default 3).
- Budget for a turn = sum of the pars of that turn's words + 5. Every clue sent costs 1 budget word; a pass costs 1 (a pass is always allowed, costs what is left if less than 1). When budget reaches 0 the describer can send no more clues; the turn ends after the current word ends. Each unused budget word at the end of the turn is +1 for the describer.
- Pass: skips the word, 0 points. If nobody gets the word, nobody scores.
- Par guard (host toggle, default off): when a word is guessed after more clues than par, every guesser except the winner loses 1 point for each clue over par (no one drops below 0).
- Buzz mode (host toggle `opts.answer = "buzz"`, default "phones" = typing): guessers buzz and say the answer out loud; the buzzer's answer goes to the judges: every player except the buzzer and the describer, voting Right or Wrong within `JUDGE_MS = 6_000`. More wrong than right is wrong; a tie or no votes is right. If there are no judges (2-player room) the describer judges. A wrong answer locks that buzzer out of this word only; buzzing reopens for the others. The buzzer has `SPEAK_MS = 5_000` to say it, then judging starts (the buzzer taps "Said it", or the clock does it).
- Teams (`opts.teams` 2+ with `opts.teamMap`): only used when every team has 2 or more players, otherwise the game plays without teams. With teams only the describer's team's other members can guess; the other team watches. Describer and guesser points go to the describer's team; describers alternate between teams and rotate within each team. A team's score is the sum of its members' points. Without teams everyone races.
- Drinking mode (optional): 1 sip to a describer whose clue was refused for containing the secret word itself; 1 sip to a buzzer whose answer was judged wrong. Nobody else sips.
- Decks: Easy (par 2), Medium (par 3), Hard (par 4), written by us in plain words (at least 120 words each, every word with its par and optional aliases), plus topic decks that reuse the curated Vowel Play name lists (`app/topicDraft/party/vowelWordList.json`, par 3, same ids as `VOWEL_TOPICS`). Deck ids in `setup.pools`; several decks mix.
- Room screens (`app/play/views/*`, `app/room/*`, and everything reachable from them) must never import the Say Less word data module or `vowelWordList.json`. A static import-graph test enforces it.
- Layout works at 320px with 12 seats. Drinking, copy and word lists are our own.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Never use `backend/.env.local` or the `api-local` config. Local API uses `DATABASE_URL=postgresql://localhost:5432/toppic_dev`. Never `pkill -f`.

## Rulings made while planning

- **Topic decks reuse Vowel Play lists.** The spec says "item names from the character catalogs"; the Vowel Play lists are already curated for guessability, so they replace raw catalog items. Cost if wrong: swap the source in Task 2.
- **Reveal pause.** Each word ends with a 4 s reveal (`REVEAL_MS = 4_000`) that shows the word, who got it and the points. It moves on by itself; no host action.
- **Turn end.** A turn also ends when the words for the turn are used up. The turn's budget bonus shows in the reveal of its last word.
- **Pool too small.** If the chosen decks hold fewer words than the game needs, it deals what there is and ends early.
- **Rematch.** A rematch avoids words already played in this room (`room.seen` keys `sl:<word>`), as Vowel Play does.
- **Departures.** The describer leaving ends the word with no points and moves on; guessers leaving never stalls (the word times out).

## Review Focus

1. A seat with no token, the screen, and any other player's snapshot during a word never contain the secret word or its aliases (also not in `history` until the word ends). Test in Tasks 3 and 5.
2. A clue that hides the answer in other forms: capital letters, accents, "Spider-Man" (parts "spider", "man"), "ice cream" parts, "Banana" vs clue "bananas", "cat" vs clue "catalog" (clue contains word → refused), "cat" word vs clue "ca" (under 4 letters, allowed). Test in Task 1.
3. A budget of 0 mid-word: clues refused, pass still works, the word still ends by guess or timer. Test in Task 3.
4. Describer leaves mid-turn or a team has all guessers gone. Test in Tasks 3 and 5.
5. Typed guess flooding and over-long guesses are refused at the server. Test in Task 5.
